# Product

## Register

product

## Users

MOBICOM lab members who need one place to run team projects, personal builds, study notes, research tasks, shared links, and lightweight discussion.

## Product Purpose

MOBICOM presents the lab publicly through home, members, about, and blog pages, while Mobi:ON serves as the authenticated project workspace. Success means a member can sign in, separate work by project, track tasks, keep notes, save resources, and leave short updates without leaving the site.

## Brand Personality

Precise, technical, focused. The public site can feel kinetic and experimental, but the workspace should feel calm, dense, and trustworthy.

## Anti-references

Avoid decorative SaaS dashboards, fake mockup-only productivity screens, oversized marketing hero patterns inside the workspace, unclear custom controls, and visual treatments that make repeated project work slower.

## Design Principles

- Make Mobi:ON usable before decorative.
- Keep project context visible across tasks, docs, links, and room activity.
- Prefer inline editing and progressive controls over modal-heavy flows.
- Preserve MOBICOM's technical identity without making the workspace noisy.
- Use motion only to confirm state changes or help orientation.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target practical WCAG AA contrast for text and controls. Respect reduced-motion preferences, keep keyboard focus visible, and make form labels or aria labels available for compact controls.
